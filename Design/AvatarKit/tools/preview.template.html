States for the agent lifecycle

      

        
idleWaiting for work. Breathes, blinks, small motion.

        
listeningUser is typing or the mic is open. Leans in, pulse ring.

        
thinkingReasoning or running tools. Looks up, thought bubble.

        
waitingBlocked on the user, like an approval. Glances around, alert badge.

        
talkingStreaming a reply. Mouth moves, body chatters.

        
happyTask finished. Hops, closed-eye smile, sparkles.

        
sleepingOffline or paused. Eyes closed, floating Z's.
